Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers to help you bank smarter.

Is Foresight Microfinance Bank licensed and secure?

Yes. Foresight Microfinance Bank Limited is fully licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN). All eligible deposits are insured by the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC).

How fast can I get loan approval?

Loan processing depends on meeting our credit requirements and completing the necessary documentation. Once your application is complete, our team will review it and communicate the next steps.

What accounts can I open with Foresight Microfinance Bank?

We offer a range of savings and business banking solutions designed to meet the needs of individuals, entrepreneurs, cooperatives, and businesses.

Are there any hidden maintenance fees?

We believe in transparent banking. Applicable charges and fees are communicated clearly to customers in accordance with our terms and conditions.

How do I open an account?

Visit any of our branches with a valid means of identification, utility bill, passport photographs, and your Bank Verification Number (BVN).

How quickly can I obtain a loan?

Loan processing depends on meeting our credit requirements and completing the necessary documentation. Our team will guide you through the process and provide an update once your application has been assessed.

Are deposits insured?

Yes. Eligible deposits are insured by the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC).

Can businesses apply for loans?

Yes. We provide financing solutions for SMEs, cooperatives, schools, traders, and registered businesses, subject to our applicable credit requirements and documentation.
