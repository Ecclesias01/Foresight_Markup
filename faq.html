Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers to help you bank smarter.

Is Foresight Microfinance Bank licensed and secure?

Yes. Foresight Microfinance Bank Limited is fully licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN). All eligible deposits are insured by the Nigerian Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC).

How fast can I get loan approval?

Once you complete our 5-minute online application, our team reviews your documentation and provides loan approvals within 24 to 48 hours.

What accounts can I open with Foresight Microfinance Bank?

We offer High-Yield Savings Accounts, Zero-Fee Current Accounts, Fixed Deposits, and dedicated SME Business Accounts.

Are there any hidden maintenance fees?

No. We provide transparent banking with no fine print or hidden account maintenance charges.
