Personal Banking

Seven account types built around the way Nigerians actually save — flexible, goal-driven, and secure.

🏦

Regular Savings Account

Min. balance: ₦500

A flexible account for individuals who want to save regularly while maintaining full access to their funds anytime.

🎯

Target Savings Account

Min. balance: ₦1,000

Set a savings goal and a target date. Designed to help you stay disciplined with structured contributions towards rent, fees, or assets.

👶

Children's Savings Account

Min. balance: ₦500

Build a financial foundation for your child from day one. Operate in the parent's name until the child comes of age.

🎓

Education Plus Account

Min. balance: ₦2,000

Purpose-built for school fees and educational expenses. Helps parents plan ahead with flexible periodic contributions.

💼

Entrepreneur Development Account

Min. balance: ₦5,000

A savings solution created specifically for business owners, traders, and artisans looking to build cash reserves.

⚡

Daily Contribution Account (FED)

Min. balance: ₦0

Daily savings collection service for traders, artisans, transport operators, and market women right at their shop or stand.

🔄

Esusu Contribution Account

Min. balance: ₦500

Digitised rotating savings (thrift) scheme for individuals and associations. Formalise your traditional esusu securely.
