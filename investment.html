Who We Are

A Trusted Partner, A Bigger Vision.

Foresight Asset Management was established to extend the Foresight promise of empowerment beyond everyday banking and into long-term wealth creation.

As a partner organization to Foresight Microfinance Bank Limited — a bank licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and regulated with deposits insured by the NDIC — we inherit a foundation built on integrity, sound risk management, and personalized service.

While Foresight Microfinance Bank focuses on savings, loans, and everyday banking for individuals, entrepreneurs, and cooperatives, Foresight Asset Management is where that same relationship grows further: helping clients put their money to work through carefully managed investment products designed for steady, sustainable returns.

Together, the two organizations form a single Foresight relationship — one trusted partner for banking today and wealth-building for tomorrow.
